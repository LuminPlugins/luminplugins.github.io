Background Audio for iOS

Your Unity app's audio stops the moment the iPhone locks. This fixes it with one line of C#. Playback runs through Apple's native AVPlayer, so it keeps going when the screen locks or the user switches apps, with real lock screen and Control Center controls, just like Podcasts or Spotify.

Lock Screen & Control Center Seek & ±30 s Skip Streaming & Local Files Call & Headphone Interruptions Zero Xcode Setup Unity 2022.3 & Unity 6

Unity 2022.3 LTS or Unity 6, iOS 14+. Works with Domain Reload disabled. Two demo scenes with TextMeshPro UI.

One-time purchase. See the current price on the Asset Store.

Background Audio for Android

The same fix for Android. Your track plays in a native foreground service, so it keeps going when the phone locks or the player switches apps, with play and pause on the lock screen and in the notification shade. Start it with one line of C#; progress callbacks drive your own Unity UI.

Lock Screen & Notification Controls Native Foreground Service Streams from a URL Audio Focus & Headphone Unplug Progress Callbacks Unity 2022.3 & Unity 6

Built for and shipped in a live app. Needs a custom Gradle template with the ExoPlayer dependency; the included setup guide walks through it.

Force Updater

Shipped a critical fix? Make sure every player is on it. Force Updater checks the installed version against a minimum you set remotely. Builds that are too old get a blocking "Update required" screen with a button straight to the App Store or Google Play, or a dismissable "recommended" prompt. Change the minimum at any time, with no app resubmission.

Required & Recommended Tiers JSON, Unity Remote Config or Firebase Reads the Real Build Version Offline-Safe Cache Re-check on Resume Editor Simulation

Unity 2022.3 or newer (Unity 6 supported). No third-party SDK bundled: host a small JSON file, or plug in Unity Remote Config or Firebase.

Review Kit

Ask for reviews at the right moment, the way Apple and Google allow. Review Kit shows the native review card on iOS and Android, but only when your timing rules say the player is ready: enough sessions, enough days, enough positive moments, and not too often. Test the whole flow in the Editor with a simulated card and a rules debugger.

Native Review Card Smart Timing Rules Rules Debugger Editor Simulated Card "Rate Us" Store Links No External Dependency Manager

Policy-safe by design: no "Enjoying the app?" pre-prompt and no review gating. Unity 2022.3 or newer (Unity 6 supported).

Sign-In + Account Deletion

Native sign-in, and the account deletion the stores require. Sign in with Google through Android Credential Manager (and the system browser on iOS), Sign in with Apple through AuthenticationServices, and a resumable account deletion flow with Apple token revocation. Works with Firebase Auth, Unity Authentication or your own server.

Sign in with Google Sign in with Apple Resumable Account Deletion Apple Token Revocation Firebase, Unity Auth or HTTP Editor Mock Provider

Needs a Google Cloud project with OAuth clients, an Apple Developer account, and a small HTTPS endpoint for Apple token revocation (templates included). Unity 2022.3 or newer.

Play Compliance Checker

Find out why Google Play will reject your build before you upload it, and which plugin is to blame. It scans your project, the Gradle output of your last Android build and the finished APK/AAB for 16 KB page size and zip alignment, target API level, Play Billing and manifest issues, then lists every problem with the file, the owner and the fix.

16 KB & Zip Alignment Scans the Built APK/AAB Names the Vendor & Fix Target API & Play Billing Markdown & JSON Reports CI Mode

Editor-only: nothing is added to your builds. Unity 2022.3 or newer with Android Build Support. Pure C#, no NDK, bundletool or Python needed.
